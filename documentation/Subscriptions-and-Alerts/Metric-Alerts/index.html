Metric Alerts

About 2208 wordsAbout 7 min

2026-11-15

A metric alert checks one measure on a schedule and sends a message only when its condition is met: Net Sales last month below 500,000, Paid Orders down more than 10% on the previous month, no new data for 3 days. It does not open a dashboard and does not need the render service; it runs a query and compares numbers.

Where to create an alert

  • From a chart. In a dashboard, hover the chart, click ⋮ and choose Set alert. The editor starts with the chart's measure, its time field and the member filters saved in the chart. Selections made in the dashboard's filter controls are not carried over; add them under More settings › Filters if the alert should watch that slice.
  • From the Metrics Library. Open a metric and click Set alert. When the metric is bound to several models, pick one first. You choose the time field yourself.
  • From the AI assistant. In a dashboard, ask for example tell me when net sales drop more than 10% month on month. The assistant answers with a suggestion card; Create opens this editor with what it understood filled in and highlights anything it could not work out, such as the measure or the threshold. The top of the editor repeats the suggestion, and Period is marked Check this until you change it: the period and filters named in the sentence are not filled in for you, so set them before saving.

Administrators and Creators can create alerts (20 per Creator by default). Readers can create restricted alerts only.

The editor

Measure

Shown as the first row only when the editor opens without a measure: from Set alert on a chart with several measures, or when the AI assistant could not tell which one you meant (The AI assistant could not pin down the measure (you said "…"); pick it here). When the chart has two to four measures they appear as buttons, with the model's other measures in the Other measure list beside them; otherwise there is one list, Choose a measure. Nothing is selected for you; saving without a measure marks the field. Otherwise the measure is under More settings.

Name

Generated from the measure and the first condition, such as Net Sales below 2,500,000.00, and kept in step with the condition until you type your own.

Period

Two choices: the time field to look along, and which period to evaluate.

The time field list starts with the fields Used in this chart, then the model's other fields. The level you pick sets the grain: day, week, month, quarter or year.

The period is counted on the calendar from the day of the check, in your tenant's time zone:

GrainCalendar choicesDefault
DayYesterday, Today (so far)Yesterday
WeekLast week, This week (to date)Last week
MonthLast month, This month (to date)Last month
QuarterLast quarter, This quarter (to date)Last quarter
YearLast year, This year (to date)This year

The default for day, week, month and quarter is the last complete period. A period still in progress only holds part of its data, so a threshold such as below 500,000 on This month would fire on the 2nd of every month.

Every grain also offers Latest month with data (the word follows the grain): the most recent period that has data, whatever today's date. Use it for models whose data stops before today, such as a monthly load that arrives weeks later or a historical sample.

Below the field, Currently: 2026-10 names the period the alert would evaluate today. No data yet for 2026-10 means the model has nothing for that period yet; threshold and change conditions are then not met. The hint then offers Use the latest month with data; or keep the calendar period and add No new data for if a missing load should alert you.

When the field's values are not recognizable as dates (month names without a year, quarter numbers such as Q3, keys like 2026 Q3), the editor says This time field isn't recognized as dates and falls back to Latest month with data and The month before that. These follow the data, not the calendar, so a load that stopped in September keeps being evaluated as September. Prefer a date-based field when the model has one.

Condition

ChoiceMeaningFill in
Above, BelowThe value of the period is above or below a numberThe number
Outside rangeThe value is outside a rangeLower and upper bound
vs. previous month (the label follows the grain)Change against the period beforechanges by over, drops by over or rises by over, and a percentage

Unusual movementThe value is far from the mean of the last 14 periodsSensitivity: low, medium or high (3, 2.5 or 2 standard deviations)

Add condition adds another one (up to 5); with two or more, choose All conditions are met or Any condition is met.

A change condition needs a comparison value. When the comparison period has no data or is 0, the condition is treated as not met and the run says No data for the comparison period. Comparing a period in progress with a complete one, for example This year (to date) against last year, compares part of a year with a whole year; set the period to the last complete one for a fair comparison.

As soon as the condition is complete, the editor runs it against current data and shows the result under the conditions: Now 2,051,342.07 (2026) · With current data: would trigger or would not trigger. Nothing is saved or sent by this check.

Severity

Info, Warning or Critical. It appears in the subject of the message, as in [Warning] Net Sales below 1,500,000 · Alert triggered, and in the alert lists.

Check

How often the alert is evaluated:

  • Hourly, on the hour, All day or only Between two times (for example 08:00 to 20:00).
  • Daily, Weekly (on the weekdays you tick) or Monthly (on a day of the month), at a time you set.

Next: shows the next check in your tenant's time zone. Checking more often than the data is loaded only repeats the same result; for a model refreshed nightly, Daily after the load is enough.

Send to and Post to channels

The same as for subscriptions: people and roles, each person receiving the message the way they chose in My account, and channels for a shared message. See Send to and Post to channels.

An alert is evaluated with your permissions and every recipient gets the same result; the message says so. People who cannot use the model, or open the report the alert was created from, are skipped and listed under the field before you save. You can only save an alert on a model you can use yourself.

More settings

SettingWhat it does
Model, MeasureThe model is fixed when the alert came from a chart or a metric; the measure can be changed.
FiltersRestrict the measure to some members, for example Channel = Online. Up to 10 filters.
Check separatelyChoose a field, such as Region or Store, to evaluate the condition for each of its members. The alert fires when any member meets the condition, and the message lists the members that did. Only the first 200 members are checked. Each member has its own state, so a second region going below the line is reported even while the first is still in alarm.
NotificationsNotify once when it becomes abnormal (default): one message when the condition starts being met, nothing more while it stays met. Keep reminding while abnormal, every N hours (up to 168): a still abnormal message at that interval. Notify on recovery (on by default) sends a message when the condition stops being met.
Attach AI analysisWhen the alert fires, the AI assistant breaks the change down by the dimensions you choose (up to 3; leave them empty to let the assistant choose) and the breakdown is added to the message. It waits up to 3 minutes; if the analysis is not finished, the alert is sent anyway with a note, and the result appears in the alert history. The breakdown shows which parts contributed to the change, not why it happened. Needs the AI extension and counts against a site-wide daily budget; the option is not shown when the budget is 0 or the AI assistant cannot take the task.
Check resultThe detailed result of the live check: the period and comparison period with their values, the change, which conditions matched, and with Check separately a table per member.

Changing the measure, the filters or the conditions of a saved alert resets its state to normal when you save, so the next check that meets the condition notifies again.

How an alert decides to send

Before the checkCondition metResultSent
NormalYesEntered alarmYes
In alarmYesStill in alarmOnly with Keep reminding, once the interval has passed (otherwise recorded as Cooldown)
In alarmNoRecoveredIf Notify on recovery is on
NormalNoNormalNo

A silenced alert is still evaluated and its state still changes; messages are held back until the silence ends, and runs show Silenced.

What recipients receive

The default subject is [Severity] Alert name · status, for example [Critical] Net Sales below 1,500,000 · Alert triggered or [Critical] Net Sales below 1,500,000 · Recovered. The message names the metric and gives the current value and period, the comparison value and change, the condition that matched, the filters, and with Check separately the members that matched. Then come the AI breakdown if attached, and these links:

  • Open report, when the alert was created from a dashboard.
  • Ask the AI assistant opens the assistant with a question about the change, to keep investigating.
  • Silence for 24 hours opens a confirmation page; confirming it silences the alert for all its recipients for 24 hours, and the page then says until when and who silenced it. The link works without signing in for the owner and for people added to Send to by name, and expires after 7 days.

The footer says the result was calculated with the owner's permissions, and in messages to a person adds a Manage what I receive link, where they can stop receiving the alert. Administrators can change the wording through the alert message template.

Restricted alerts for Readers

Readers can set an alert on a chart they can see, for themselves only:

  • Start from the chart's ⋮ › Set alert in the dashboard. The measure, filters and time field come from the chart and cannot be changed; the condition, check, notifications and severity can.
  • The alert is sent only to the Reader; other people, roles and channels cannot be added, and Check separately is not available.
  • Filters set with the dashboard's filter controls are not included, so the value can differ from what the dashboard shows at that moment.
  • If the report or the chart changes so that it no longer matches the alert, the alert is turned off and its history says The report or component changed; set it up again from the chart.
  • A Reader can have up to 5 restricted alerts by default; administrators can lower the limit or set it to 0 to turn the feature off.

Restricted alerts are marked Restricted in the alert lists.
