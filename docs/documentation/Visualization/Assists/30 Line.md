# Lines and arrows

Lines and arrows are types of the **Shape** component.

1. Add **Components → Assists → Shape**.
2. In **Style → Shape → Type**, choose **Line**, **Arrow**, or **Double arrow**.
3. Adjust the line appearance under **Fill and line**, and use **Rotate** and the component’s size to position it.

Use lines to separate sections and arrows to explain a sequence. Keep them clear of chart labels and interactive controls.
